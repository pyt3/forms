# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Laboratory quality staff and supervisors use the app during daily operations to record check form data, review what has been submitted, and prepare monthly reports without extra manual rework.

## Product Purpose

This tool helps the team submit the daily check form quickly and accurately, then turn that data into a monthly report in the same format the team already needs for internal control. Success means faster submission, fewer errors, and a report that is easier to produce and trust.

## Positioning

A web app that makes daily quality checks easy to enter and converts them into monthly reports with minimal manual effort.

## Brand Personality

Clean, easy to use, and focused on best-in-class UX/UI.

## Anti-references

A plain internal form that feels bare, hard to scan, or unfinished.

## Design Principles

- Make the daily submission path short, obvious, and forgiving.
- Keep the reporting workflow aligned with the internal controlled form format the team expects.
- Prioritize clarity and speed over decorative complexity.
- Show only the information needed for the current task so the interface stays calm under daily use.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use a general accessibility baseline with strong contrast, keyboard-friendly interaction, readable labels, and sensible focus states. No special accessibility requirements were requested beyond standard usability.
